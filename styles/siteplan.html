Salt Lake City Chamber of Commerce – Site Plan

Site Name

Salt Lake City Chamber of Commerce

Site Purpose

The Salt Lake City Chamber of Commerce website serves as a central hub for local businesses, entrepreneurs, and community members. The website provides resources for business growth, promotes economic development, highlights local events, and encourages networking and collaboration among businesses in the Salt Lake City area.

Target Market

The primary audience for the Salt Lake City Chamber of Commerce website includes local business owners, entrepreneurs, and companies seeking to grow their presence in Salt Lake City.

Secondary audiences include businesses considering relocating to Salt Lake City, professionals seeking networking opportunities, and community members interested in local events and economic development.

Site Goals

  • Support local businesses by providing chamber membership information, resources, and promotional opportunities.
  • Promote economic growth in Salt Lake City by connecting businesses, residents, and visitors through events and business listings.
  • Strengthen the chamber’s reputation as a trusted source for networking, advocacy, and community development.

Scenarios

What networking events are available for local businesses in Salt Lake City this month?

How can my business become a member of the Salt Lake City Chamber of Commerce?

What resources are available to help new businesses start or relocate to Salt Lake City?

Color Schema

Navy Blue (#1D3557): Used for headers and navigation to create a professional and trustworthy look.

Gold (#E9C46A): Used for accents, buttons, and highlights.

Light Gray (#F1F1F1): Used for page backgrounds and section backgrounds.

Typography

Heading Font: Montserrat, sans-serif

Body Font: Open Sans, sans-serif

User Personas

Small Business Owner – Maria Lopez

Maria owns a small bakery in Salt Lake City and wants to grow her business. She visits the chamber website to find networking events, learn about local business programs, and explore promotional opportunities.

Entrepreneur – David Chen

David recently moved to Salt Lake City and is launching a technology startup. He visits the chamber website to connect with other entrepreneurs and learn about resources that support new businesses.

SEO Plan

  • Use relevant keywords such as Salt Lake City Chamber of Commerce, Salt Lake City business networking, and Salt Lake City business events.
  • Verify the chamber on Google's Business Profile to improve local search visibility.
  • Gain backlinks from member businesses and community organizations.
  • Use Google Analytics to monitor website traffic and visitor behavior.

Site Map

  • Home
  • Discover Salt Lake City
  • Directory
  • Join

The homepage will serve as the central hub linking to the Discover page, Business Directory, and Membership page.
