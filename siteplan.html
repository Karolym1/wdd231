BSCPhotoPro – Site Plan

Site Name

BSCPhotoPro

This name represents the business brand and clearly communicates that the website is focused on professional content creation, branding, and marketing support for small businesses.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help small businesses improve their online presence through social media management, content creation, branding, and marketing strategy. The site will provide service information, helpful business support, and a way for potential clients to contact the business.

Scenarios

What services does BSCPhotoPro offer to help my business grow online?

How can I contact BSCPhotoPro to get help with social media, branding, or content creation?

Color Schema

Black (#000000): Used for headings, main text, and to create a clean professional look.

Gold (#C9A227): Used for accents, borders, and highlights to give the website a premium feel.

Light Gray (#F5F5F5): Used as the page background to keep the layout bright and easy to read.

Typography

Montserrat: Used for headings and section titles.

Open Sans: Used for body text and general page content.
